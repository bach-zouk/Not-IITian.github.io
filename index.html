Abhishek Sharma

From cats & dogs to proteins & cells: a large-scale ML/AI perspective

I am an ML researcher currently focused on understanding how biological systems maintain their identity, respond to perturbations, and transition between states. I am particularly interested in cell reprogramming, with the long-term (optimistic) goal of applying it to cancer and brain diseases. An underlying theme of my ML research centres around simple unified frameworks, learning from constraints and weak supervision.

I did my PhD at Ecole Polytechnique and MS in Math, Vision and Learning. I got interested in Science while doing my bachelor's at CUSAT, India.
